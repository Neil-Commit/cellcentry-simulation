ABOUT THIS SIMULATION

This portal is a purely front-end, in-browser simulation built to illustrate, for a pitch or judging session, what a CellSentry PH municipal monitoring dashboard could show. It intentionally imitates the visual style of older Philippine government web portals for demonstration flavor.

What this IS:

  • A scripted, in-memory model of the CellSentry failover state machine (PRIMARY_HEALTHY → PRIMARY_DEGRADED → BACKUP_ACTIVE → BOTH_DEGRADED → OFFLINE → RECOVERY_PENDING).
  • An interactive way to raise or clear simulated "congestion" on Tower A or Tower B and see health scores, latency, and packet loss respond, and the site fail over automatically once congestion is severe and sustained.

What this is NOT:

  • Not connected to any real modem, SIM, carrier, or cell tower.
  • Not an official Philippine government website, and not affiliated with any specific real LGU, DRRMO, or the DICT.
  • Not a source of real emergency advisories — all "advisory" content on the Home tab is placeholder text.
  • Data resets on page reload; nothing here is stored server-side.

Who made this:

  • A group of students from Far Eastern University Institute of Technology for the DICT Philippine Startup Challenge (CellSentry PH).
